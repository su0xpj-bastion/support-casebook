`dsqa_slice.jsonl` is a 60-item stratified slice (seed 2026) of DeepSearchQA by Google DeepMind,
https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/deepsearchqa at commit `b2623f8653065c2672de6d941fc5434cd652376c` (file `DSQA-full.csv`, 900 rows).
Licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Fields are unchanged; `id` is the row index in the source file.
`answer_type` is used only for grading and is never sent to a model, as the dataset card requires.
