# AN-33 — IdP cert expired with Require SSO on: whole org locked out

**Scope:** protocol behaviour (SAML / OAuth / OIDC), independent of any vendor.

Contact Support from an Owner/Primary Owner email; then update the cert and Test sign-in. Track IdP cert expiry.
